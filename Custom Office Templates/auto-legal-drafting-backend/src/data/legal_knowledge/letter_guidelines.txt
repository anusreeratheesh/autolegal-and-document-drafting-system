FORMAL LETTER WRITING GUIDELINES

HEADER FORMAT
- **Sender's Info**: Top Left (Name, Address, Contact).
- **Date**: Below Sender's Info, usually with a gap.
- **Recipient's Info**: Below Date, Left aligned (Name/Title, Company, Address).
- **Subject Line**: Clear, concise, and bold. Starts with "Subject:".

SALUTATIONS
- Formal: "Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name]," or "Dear [Full Name],"
- Unknown Recipient: "To Whom It May Concern," or "Dear Sir/Madam,"

OPENING
State the purpose of the letter immediately in the first paragraph. Be direct but polite.
Example: "I am writing to formally request..." or "I am writing to resign from my position as..."

BODY PARAGRAPHS
- Keep paragraphs short and focused on a single point.
- Use professional and neutral tone.
- Avoid slang or overly casual language.
- Maintain clarity and brevity.

CLOSING
Reiterate the main point or call to action. express gratitude.
Example: "Thank you for your time and consideration." or "I look forward to your positive response."

SIGN-OFF
- Formal: "Sincerely," or "Respectfully," or "Yours Application,"
- Slightly less formal: "Best regards," or "Kind regards,"
- Follow with your signature and printed name.

COMMON LETTER TYPES
- **Resignation**: State intention to resign, last working day, and offer help during transition.
- **Cover Letter**: Highlight relevant skills and match them to the job description.
- **Complaint**: State the problem clearly, provide evidence (dates, details), and state desired resolution.
- **Recommendation**: Explain relationship, highlight strengths with examples, and give a strong endorsement.
