PROFESSIONAL EMAIL GUIDELINES

SUBJECT LINE
- Crucial for open rates.
- Must be specific and descriptive.
- Bad: "Hi" or "Question"
- Good: "Meeting Request: Project X Review" or "Invoice #1234 for March Services"

SALUTATION
- Professional: "Dear [Name]," or "Hi [Name],"
- Team: "Hi Team," or "Dear All,"
- Avoid: "Hey guys," (too casual for formal contexts).

THE BODY (The 5 Ss)
1. **Short**: Respect the reader's time.
2. **Simple**: Use plain English.
3. **Structured**: Use bullet points for lists.
4. **Specific**: Get to the point.
5. **Soft**: Be polite, even when firm.

TONE
- Avoid typing in ALL CAPS (shouting).
- Be careful with humor/sarcasm (often lost in text).
- Use positive language ("I can do that by Friday" vs "I can't do that until Friday").

CALL TO ACTION (CTA)
- Be clear about what you want the recipient to do.
- "Please let me know by Tuesday if this works."
- "Please approve the attached draft."

SIGNATURE
- Professional closing ("Best regards,").
- Full Name.
- Title/Position.
- Contact Information (Phone, Website, etc.).

ATTACHMENTS
- Mention the attachment in the body ("Please find attached...").
- Ensure files are named clearly (e.g., "Contract_Draft_v1.pdf" vs "Scan001.pdf").
